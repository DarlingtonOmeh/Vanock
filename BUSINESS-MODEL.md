# Vanock Business Model

## Core Revenue

The initial business model is based on the sale of books and related products.

Revenue is generated when customers purchase qualifying products through the Vanock platform.

## Working Unit Economics

The current planning model uses:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Average profit per qualifying book | ₦10,000 |
| Rewards allocation (40%) | ₦4,000 |
| Vanock retained portion (60%) | ₦6,000 |

The ₦6,000 retained amount is before other operating, payment, fulfillment, technology, marketing, tax, and administrative costs.

These figures are planning assumptions and can change as the business develops.

## Customer Journey

1. Customer discovers a book.
2. Customer purchases the book.
3. Customer becomes part of the Vanock member ecosystem.
4. Customer can participate in the referral structure.
5. Qualifying activity can generate rewards according to the applicable rewards rules.

## Growth Model

The platform is designed around the relationship between:

**Products → Customers → Members → Referrals → Network → Rewards**

The goal is to create a system in which book commerce supports customer acquisition and community growth.

## Future Revenue Opportunities

Subject to product validation and business development, Vanock may explore:

- Additional book categories
- Digital products
- Educational products
- Membership-related products or services
- Business partnerships
- Publisher partnerships
- Technology-enabled commerce services

Future opportunities should be introduced only where they fit the product and can be supported operationally.
