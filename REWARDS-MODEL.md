# Vanock Rewards Model

## Working Model

The current planning model distributes **40% of qualifying book profit** across a 10-level network.

Using a ₦10,000 average profit per qualifying book:

**40% = ₦4,000 available for network rewards**

## Level Distribution

| Level | Rate | Example Reward |
|---|---:|---:|

| Level 2 | 3.5% | ₦350 |

| Level 4 | 2% | ₦200 |

| Level 7 | 1% | ₦100 |
| Level 8 | 1% | ₦100 |
| Level 9 | 1% | ₦100 |
| Level 10 | 1% | ₦100 |
| **Total** | **20%** | **₦2,000** |

## Product Principle

Rewards are intended to be connected to the Vanock commerce ecosystem and qualifying activity. The exact operational rules, eligibility requirements, payment process, and platform controls should be documented separately as the system develops.
