# Vanock User Flow

## Current Simplified Flow

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
BOOK / PRODUCT
   ↓
PURCHASE
   ↓
MEMBERSHIP
   ↓
REFERRAL
   ↓
NETWORK
   ↓
QUALIFYING ACTIVITY
   ↓
REWARDS
```

## Step 1 — Discover

A potential customer discovers Vanock through the website, social media, referrals, content, advertising, or other channels.

## Step 2 — Select a Product

The customer reviews available books or other qualifying products.

## Step 3 — Purchase

The customer completes the applicable purchase process using the payment options supported by the platform.

## Step 4 — Membership

A qualifying purchase can place the customer into the Vanock member ecosystem according to the applicable platform rules.

## Step 5 — Referral

A member can introduce other people to Vanock using the platform's referral process.

## Step 6 — Network

Referrals can create relationships across multiple levels of the member network.

## Step 7 — Rewards

Where activity meets the applicable rules, rewards can be calculated according to the Vanock rewards model.

## Future Automated Flow

The long-term product can automate:

- Account creation
- Referral assignment
- Purchase verification
- Network placement
- Reward calculation
- Reward history
- Member notifications
- Administrative reporting
