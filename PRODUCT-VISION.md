# Vanock Product Vision

## The Vision

Vanock aims to build a digital commerce ecosystem where books are more than products to purchase. They become an entry point into a connected community and rewards experience.

### Core idea

**Buy. Connect. Share. Earn.**

## Product Pillars

### 1. Books

Vanock provides a marketplace for selected books and digital products that can help customers learn, grow, and discover new opportunities.

### 2. Commerce

The platform provides a structured environment for product discovery, purchasing, customer accounts, and future digital commerce capabilities.

### 3. Community

Customers can become members of a connected ecosystem rather than remaining one-time purchasers.

### 4. Rewards

The platform can allocate a defined portion of qualifying profit to member rewards according to its published reward structure.

### 5. Technology

Over time, Vanock can automate more of the customer journey, including registration, purchases, referrals, reward calculations, dashboards, notifications, reporting, and administration.

## Long-Term Direction

Potential future capabilities include:

- Member dashboard
- Automated referral tracking
- Automated reward calculations
- Order and transaction history
- Digital product delivery
- Admin analytics
- Notifications
- Payment integrations
- APIs and third-party integrations
- Mobile-friendly member experience
- Data-driven product and community insights

## Important Product Principle

Vanock should grow from a functioning commerce platform into a technology platform based on actual customer usage and validated demand.

The current implementation may use third-party technologies and templates. The product vision and business model are documented here independently of those components.
